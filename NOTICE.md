# Sources and credits

This independent Minecraft 1.12.2 Cleanroom backport is based on Technici4n/Powah,
branch 1.21.1, pinned by upstream-lock.json. Powah was created by owmii and is
maintained by Technici4n and contributors. Original code and assets are distributed
under LGPL-3.0, included in LICENSE. Texture credits: Cyn and SammySemicolon.

Models, textures, translations and recipe data are adapted from that checkout.
Modern vanilla blue ice and deepslate textures are Minecraft assets owned by
Mojang/Microsoft. Their corresponding backfill resources are only used in Minecraft.
Ponder is an optional, separately distributed dependency; its code is not bundled.

The native guide reader adapts the layout and colors of AppliedEnergistics/GuideME
21.1.7 (LGPL-3.0), and includes its original background and toolbar icon textures.
GuideME provenance and artifact checksums are recorded in guide-ui-lock.json.
The 28 guidebook pages and illustrations are imported from the pinned Powah source.
